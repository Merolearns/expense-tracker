Recurring charges

Rent, subscriptions, the gym — the charges that quietly eat the budget every month. A charge counts as recurring when the same amount in the same category shows up in 3 or more of the last 6 months. Amounts are compared to the cent, so a subscription that bumps its price stops matching and stands out instead of hiding in an average.
